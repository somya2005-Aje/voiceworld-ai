FULL DESCRIPTION:
Turn your text into natural human speech with VoiceWorld AI.

Type or paste any text, pick one of 84 real voices across 28 languages —
including Hindi, English (US, UK and India), Bengali, Telugu, Tamil,
Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and more — and hear it
spoken in a clear, natural voice. Every voice has a real audio sample,
so you can listen before you generate.

Generations go up to 40,000 words. One short rewarded video ad unlocks
each generation, on every tier — 5,000, 10,000, 20,000, 25,000, 30,000,
35,000 or 40,000 words. Long text is split into sentences automatically,
so even a whole story or lesson becomes one smooth audio file.

Your audio is saved to your private Library, where you can play it,
download it as a real MP3, or share it. Dark and light themes are built
in, and your history stays on your account until you delete it.

Made for creators, students, teachers and anyone who prefers listening:
narrate stories, lessons, videos and reels in the language your audience
speaks. No voice cloning, no fake voices — only real voices that exist.

Download VoiceWorld AI and give your words a voice.
