Privacy Policy

VoiceWorld AI turns your text into natural human speech. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app collects, why it is needed, and how you can delete it.

Effective date: 11 November 2026  •  Last updated: 11 November 2026  •  Developer: VoiceWorld AI (individual developer)  •  Contact: branjandharua7@gmail.com

1. What data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data, and we do not share your audio or your text with advertisers.

1.1 Account information

When you create an account, we store your display name and email address through Firebase Authentication (Google). This is used only to sign you in and to keep your library private to your account.

1.2 Text you enter and audio you generate

  • The text you type is sent to our voice-generation server (a Cloudflare Worker) only to produce the audio you asked for. The server does not keep your text, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone.
  • The audio that is generated, and its details (title, voice, language, word count, date), is saved in your private library in our database (Google Firestore). Only your account can see it.
  • The app uses preset voices only. It does not record your voice and does not clone voices.

1.3 App settings

Your preferences (theme, language, selected voice, generation limits) are stored on your device and, where needed, in your account, so the app remembers them.

1.4 Advertising

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as your advertising ID, and general device information, to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings at any time. The web version of VoiceWorld AI shows no third-party ads.

1.5 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or files.
  • We do not read your messages or other apps.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data.

2. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your account and library private.
  • To generate the speech audio you request, in the voice and language you choose.
  • To save your generated audio in your library so you can play, download, or share it later.
  • To show ads in the Android app (AdMob), which keeps the app free.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

3. Third-party services

VoiceWorld AI uses these services to run. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Workers (voice-generation server) — Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads in the Android app) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech (the voice engine behind generation) — your text is processed to create audio, as described in section 1.2.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Your audio: you can delete any item from your Library at any time, inside the app.
  • Your account: you can delete your whole account from Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your account and your library data. Some data may remain in routine backups for a short time and is then removed automatically.
  • If you only log out, your account and library stay saved for when you return.

5. Children's privacy

VoiceWorld AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has created an account, contact us at branjandharua7@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Security

All traffic to our servers uses HTTPS. Your library is stored under your own account and is protected by Firebase security rules, so other users cannot see it. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the app's data handling as small as possible: we only store what the app needs to work.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time, in the app: view and delete your generated audio, change your display name, and delete your account. To ask for a copy of your data, or to ask a privacy question, email branjandharua7@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change, and the new version will be posted on this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, contact: branjandharua7@gmail.com.
