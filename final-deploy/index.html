FAQ

Do I need to watch an ad to generate?

Yes — every generation starts with one short rewarded video ad. One ad unlocks one generation, on every tier up to 40,000 words. While we are in development the ads are Google’s official test ads.

Do I need Google Sign-In?

The app offers email signup/login and “Continue with Google” via Firebase Auth (project voiceworld-ai). Both are real logins — sign in with Google or email.

Where is the Android APK?

Published — tap “Download App” above to get the Android APK (v1.4.4). The web app at “Start Creating” works in your browser today.

Which languages actually work?

It depends on the voices installed on your device/browser. The app lists every target language, enables the ones with a real voice, and marks the rest “not available in this browser”.

Can I download my audio as MP3?

Yes — audio is generated on the server as a real MP3 in your selected voice, and the Download button saves that MP3 file.

Privacy & Consent — first, not last

Your generated audio is private to you. You can delete your audio and your whole account from Profile. No audio leaves your device except what you choose to share. Full terms and privacy are in the app under Profile → Privacy & Terms.

Support: in the app, go to Profile → Support. For setup questions (Firebase, AdMob, APK publishing), contact the app admin.
